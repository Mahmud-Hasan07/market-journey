# 📈 Market Journey

> **Before you risk it for real — risk it here first.**

**Market Journey** is a beginner-friendly, web-based stock market simulation game designed to help non-traders experience the excitement, uncertainty, and decision-making involved in financial markets — **without risking real money**.

Players start with **€10,000 in virtual cash** and enter a simulated market where prices move, news creates uncertainty, and decisions have consequences.

---

## 🎯 Why Market Journey?

Many people are curious about investing but find the stock market intimidating.

Market Journey provides a safe environment where beginners can **experience the market before entering the real one**.

Instead of only reading about investing, players can:

- 📊 Experience market movements
- 📰 React to simulated market news
- 💰 Manage virtual money
- 📈 Build and monitor a portfolio
- 🧠 Make buy, hold, and sell decisions
- ⚡ Experience uncertainty and market shocks
- 🎮 Learn through interactive gameplay

> **Same market. No real money. Real skills.**

---

## 🕹️ How It Works

### 1. Start with €10,000

Every player receives **€10,000 in virtual cash**.

### 2. Enter the market

Explore different companies and observe simulated price movements.

### 3. Make decisions

Choose whether to:

**BUY → HOLD → SELL**

Every decision can change your simulated portfolio.

### 4. Experience market events

News, rumours, market shocks and unexpected events influence the simulated market.

### 5. Learn from your decisions

Track your portfolio and understand how different decisions affect your results.

## 🎓 Educational Goals

Market Journey focuses on **experiential learning**.

The goal is not to teach users how to become professional traders.

Instead, it aims to help beginners understand fundamental concepts such as:

- Market volatility
- Risk and reward
- Portfolio management
- Diversification
- Decision-making under uncertainty
- Emotional reactions to gains and losses
- The influence of news and events
- Basic investing terminology
